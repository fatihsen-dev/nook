# Nook logo guidelines

## Idea

The second o of "nook" floats above the line, like a nooklet panel resting on the desktop. The lifted o is the only accent element; everything else stays Graphite (or Mist when reversed).

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `nook-wordmark-reversed.svg` | Primary logo, on Graphite and other dark surfaces |
| `nook-wordmark.svg` | Logo on light surfaces |
| `nook-symbol-reversed.svg`, `nook-symbol.svg` | The "oo" symbol where the wordmark does not fit (avatars, toolbar, small UI), dark and light |
| `nook-symbol-small.svg` | Heavier cut of the light symbol for 32 px and below |
| `nook-*-black.svg`, `nook-*-white.svg` | One-colour versions for single-colour printing, engraving, or busy photos |
| `nook-app-icon.svg`, `nook.icns` | macOS app icon (1024 grid, 824 tile, radius 185) |

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#161816` | 22 24 22 | Letters on light surfaces, dark surfaces, app icon tile |
| Lime | `#B8E62E` | 184 230 46 | The lifted o on dark surfaces |
| Moss | `#6E8F0C` | 110 143 12 | The lifted o on light surfaces |
| Mist | `#F2F5EC` | 242 245 236 | Letters on dark surfaces |

Nook is dark-first: prefer Graphite surfaces with Lime. Lime on white is 1.5:1 and fades, so light surfaces use Moss (3.8:1). Contrast: Lime on Graphite 12.2:1, Mist on Graphite 16.2:1, Graphite on white 17.9:1. Print (CMYK, Pantone) values are not yet matched; proof them before any print run.

## Clear space

Keep free space around the logo equal to the height of one o (the x-height). Nothing else may enter that area.

## Minimum size

- Wordmark: 72 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
- Symbol: 16 px; use `nook-symbol-small.svg` at 32 px and below.

## Do not

- Put the lifted o back on the line, or lift a different letter.
- Colour any letter other than the lifted o in Lime or Moss.
- Use Lime for the lifted o on light surfaces, or Lime as the background for the two-colour logo.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, or retype the wordmark in a font.
